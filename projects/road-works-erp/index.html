What it keeps track of

  • Fuel, cycle by cycle

    Closing levels carry into the next cycle. Top-ups and working hours produce efficiency figures, so unusual consumption stands out.

  • Approvals with a trail

    Each requisition step is recorded against a named person and time, so every approval can be traced.

  • Permissions that hold

    Access rules are enforced in the database. Someone without approval rights cannot approve or dispatch, whatever screen they use.

  • Many sites, one view

    Set up warehouses and project sites, and watch several projects from a single account.
